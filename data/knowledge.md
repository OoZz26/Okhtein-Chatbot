## About
Okhtein ("sisters" in Arabic) is an Egyptian luxury accessories house founded by sisters Aya and Mounaz Abdelraouf in Cairo.
Handcrafted bags, small leather goods, eyewear and fine jewelry, with signature brass hardware inspired by Egyptian and Arabian heritage (palmette, lotus, horseshoe motifs).

## Website
- Egypt store: https://eg.okhtein.com
- Prices on the Egypt store are in EGP.

## Orders, changes & cancellation
- To change or cancel an order before it is dispatched: email orders@okhtein.com with the order number.
- After dispatch, use the returns policy below.

## Returns & refunds
- Returns accepted within 14 days of delivery, no reason needed.
- Item must be unused, with all tags, labels and protective packaging; return in the original packaging (Okhtein may refuse returns without it).
- Refunds go back to the original payment method, processed within 14 days after the return reaches the warehouse (bank may take 5-10 business days).
